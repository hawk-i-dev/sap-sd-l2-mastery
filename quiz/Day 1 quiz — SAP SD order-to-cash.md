Answer from memory. For multiple-choice questions, choose **one** answer.

1. Put these in the usual order: **billing document, sales order, goods issue, outbound delivery**.

2. A **sales area** consists of which three elements?
   A. Company code, plant, shipping point  
   B. Sales organization, distribution channel, division  
   C. Customer, material, plant

3. An order exists, but there is **no outbound delivery** in document flow. Which stage would you investigate first?

4. A delivery exists, but **goods issue has not been posted**. Has the standard physical-goods flow reached billing yet? What status would you check?

5. What does **document flow** help you establish in an L2 ticket?

6. Two orders have the same customer and material, but behave differently. Name **three other details** you would compare before assuming a system defect.

8. An invoice exists in the system, but the customer says they did not receive it. Would you first investigate **invoice creation** or **invoice output/delivery to the customer**? Explain in one sentence.

9. What is the difference between the **expected result** and the **actual result** in a support ticket? Give an example for a missing-invoice case.

10. **L2 scenario:** Ten orders failed billing today, while yesterday’s orders billed successfully. What evidence would you gather to determine whether this is an isolated order issue or a wider incident?
